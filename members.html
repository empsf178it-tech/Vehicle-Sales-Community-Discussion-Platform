HOW IT WORKS

The Reputation Architecture

Reputation on REVVOLT is a merit-based governance model designed to reward diagnostic accuracy, thoroughness, and courteous community support.

Verified Helpful Votes

Every time another member upvotes your answer as the solution to their mechanical or purchasing problem, you earn +10 reputation points.

Proof-Backed Reviews

Submitting long-term ownership reviews accompanied by actual maintenance invoices, dyno sheets, or alignment printouts grants +50 points.

Community Moderation Rights

Members achieving 1,000+ reputation points receive community moderation privileges, including the ability to pin verified answers and audit listings.
